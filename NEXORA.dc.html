About

Patterned in 3D,
homegrown in India

NEXORA is a studio-first clothing label. Every block starts as geometry: we cut the pattern in 3D, test the drape in simulation, and only then send it to the tailors we work with outside Delhi.

That is also why every product page ships with its 3D twin instead of a slideshow. You see the garment the way we made it — turnable, measurable, honest about volume. No pinned-back photography.

The 3D Studio is the same toolchain, opened up. Give it a reference photograph and it returns a parametric garment you can turn, wireframe and publish.
